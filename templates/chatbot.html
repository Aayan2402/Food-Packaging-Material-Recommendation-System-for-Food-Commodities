Official Regulatory Sources

PackSense AI grounds food safety and packaging guidance on official statutory gazette notifications, parliamentary acts, and state enforcement directories:

1. FSSAI Packaging Regulations, 2018 (Official Gazette)

Notified under Section 92 of FSS Act, 2006. Prescribes food-grade parameters, migration limits (IS 9845), and explicitly bans newspaper, printed recycled paper, and non-certified recycled plastic for food wrapping.

Official FSSAI Portal (fssai.gov.in) →

2. Maharashtra Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

State-level enforcement authority administering inspections, mobile food testing vans, and anti-adulteration crackdowns across Maharashtra districts.

Official FDA Maharashtra Portal (fda.maharashtra.gov.in) →

3. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Food Contact Specifications

Prescribed Indian Standards: IS 10146 (Polyethylene), IS 10910 (Polypropylene), IS 12252 (PET), IS 6615 (Greaseproof paper), and IS 9845 (Overall Migration Limits).

Official BIS Repository →
