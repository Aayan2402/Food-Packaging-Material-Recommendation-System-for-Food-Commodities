Official Regulatory Sources

PackSense AI strictly cites verified statutory gazette notifications, parliamentary acts, and government portals:

1. FSSAI Packaging Regulations, 2018 (Official Gazette)

Notified under Section 92 of FSS Act, 2006. Mandates food-grade compliance, Overall Migration Limit (IS 9845), and explicitly prohibits the use of newspaper and recycled plastics for food contact.

Visit FSSAI Official Portal (fssai.gov.in) →

2. Maharashtra Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

State-level enforcement body responsible for surveillance, food sampling, seizing adulterated goods, and enforcing FSSAI hygiene rules across Maharashtra.

Visit FDA Maharashtra Portal (fda.maharashtra.gov.in) →

3. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Food Contact Specifications

Indian Standards for plastics (IS 10146, IS 10910, IS 12252), greaseproof paper (IS 6615), glass containers (IS 1382), and tinplate (IS 1997).

Visit BIS Standards Repository →
